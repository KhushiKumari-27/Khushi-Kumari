About Me

Khushi Kumari

I'm a final-year BCA student at Srinath University, Jamshedpur, with a CGPA above 9 and a genuine passion for turning data into decisions. My journey into data analytics started with curiosity — wondering why businesses fail to act on the information they already have — and quickly became a skill I've been building with purpose ever since.

During my internship at Tata Steel, I got my first real taste of professional data work: cleaning a messy industrial dispatch dataset, building a Power BI dashboard from scratch, and presenting findings through two rounds of stakeholder feedback. Seeing the dashboard actually get used by the project team was the moment I knew this was the right path.

Outside of internship work, I've independently built SQL-driven churn analyses and productivity dashboards — each one a deliberate attempt to sharpen a different part of my analytical toolkit. I'm currently deepening my skills in Python and exploring AI/ML, with my sights set on a Data Analyst role where I can contribute from day one.

📍 Jamshedpur, Jharkhand  ·  Open to remote & on-site internships
