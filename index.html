Philosophy

My Approach

"Finding the pattern that changes the decision."

I believe data is only as valuable as the clarity it creates. My process always starts with the question behind the question — understanding what a stakeholder actually needs to decide before touching a single dataset.

From there, I let the data speak: cleaning it carefully, exploring it honestly, and presenting it in a way that feels obvious in hindsight. Whether it's a SQL query or a Power BI dashboard, the goal is always the same — make the right decision easier to reach.

I'm currently in my final year of BCA at Srinath University, building real-world experience one dataset at a time.
